Just Drop is an open-source Android app for transferring files directly between devices connected to the same Wi-Fi network.

Features:

<ul> 
<li>Discover nearby devices on the local network</li> 
<li>Send multiple files between Android devices</li> 
<li>Accept or decline incoming transfers</li> 
<li>Track transfer progress</li> 
<li>Transfer files locally without cloud storage or an external server</li> <
/ul>

Requirements:

<ul> 
<li>Android 10 (API 29) or later</li> 
<li>Both devices must be connected to the same Wi-Fi network</li> 
</ul>

Source code: https://github.com/1owmark/Just-Drop
